Before you download

What is the mock-test format?

20 questions in 45 minutes. Pass with at least 15 correct answers overall and all five Australian Values questions correct. This is the app's default mock format. Practice results do not guarantee an official test pass.

Does the app include audio or translations?

No built-in audio lessons, spoken question narration or translated study content are included. The app's study content is in English.

Can I study without an internet connection?

The app stores bundled questions and downloaded question data on your device. An internet connection is needed for purchases, Restore Purchases and new content downloads. We do not promise that every feature will work offline. Before travelling, open the app online and check that the study content you need is available.

Do I need an account?

No app account or sign-in is required. Apple handles the optional Lifetime Access purchase.

What can I try for free?

Complete two mock tests before deciding whether to purchase Lifetime Access.

  • 20 questions per mock test, with a 45-minute time limit.

What does Lifetime Access include?

One in-app purchase unlocks:

  • 2,500 practice questions with topic-based and mixed practice.
  • A full study guide covering Australia and its people; democratic beliefs, rights and liberties; government and the law; and Australian values.
  • Unlimited timed mock tests.
  • Saved questions, mistake review, progress tracking and mock test history.
  • Future question-bank updates.

Practice Questions and the Study Guide require Lifetime Access. The free option includes two mock tests.

How can I use the app to prepare?

Start with the official Australian Citizenship: Our Common Bond resource linked below. Try a mock test, review your mistakes and revisit the topics you find difficult. If you choose Lifetime Access, use topic practice and the study guide alongside the official material, then check your progress with further mock tests.
